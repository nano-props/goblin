Features

Everything you need to build,
nothing in your way

Files, terminals, branches, worktrees, and pull requests — together, wherever you work.

Multiple Projects, One View

Open folders and repositories in tabs. Reorder them, pick up where you left off, and see what matters from a clear dashboard.

Local & Remote

Work with projects here or somewhere else. Your files, terminals, and Git tools feel right at home in either one.

Branches & Worktrees

See what changed and what needs attention. Pull, push, and create or remove worktrees without losing your place.

Files & Terminals

Browse and download files. Run multiple shell sessions that stay ready when you switch tabs or reconnect.

History & Pull Requests

Explore commits and changes, copy patches, and see reviews, checks, and merge status beside every linked pull request.

In your browser

Run Goblin on your server and open it in a browser. Your files and terminals stay ready across screens.

How It Works

Three steps. You're in.

Open a folder. Goblin brings the right tools with it.

1

Open a project

Enter a server folder path, clone a repository, or open a remote project. Everything you had open comes back next time.

2

See the whole picture

Move naturally between the dashboard, branches, files, changes, history, and terminals.

3

Keep moving

Connect from another browser and continue working. Your server keeps your files and terminals ready.
